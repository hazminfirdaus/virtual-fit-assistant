Client-Side Virtual Fit Assistant

This prototype estimates a generic unisex T-shirt size using browser-based pose estimation. Webcam processing occurs locally on your device.

1. Position Yourself

Face the camera directly and keep both shoulders and both hips visible. Align your torso with the positioning guide and hold still. Start the camera to begin the first analysis. To reanalyse, move out slightly from the positioning guide, then return to a valid position and hold still. A fresh analysis will begin automatically.
